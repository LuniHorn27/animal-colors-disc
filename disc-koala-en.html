What Is the S Koala Type?

The DISC behavior style test is based on a theory put forward by psychologist William Marston in his 1928 book Emotions of Normal People. It sorts behavior into four tendencies: D (Dominance), I (Influence), S (Steadiness) and C (Conscientiousness). Describing them as D Tiger, I Peacock, S Koala and C Owl is a popular shorthand added later to make them easier to remember.

Everyone has all four tendencies, just in different proportions. "S Koala type" means the S tendency is the most noticeable one, not that the person has only the S Koala side.

Strengths and Blind Spots of the S Koala Type

Strengths

  • Reliable: They keep following through on what they promise, which puts people at ease.
  • Patient: They are willing to take the time to do things well, and to wait for others.
  • Good listeners: They make people feel understood, so they are easy to be around.
  • The glue of the team: They ease tension and help everyone work together smoothly.

Blind spots

  • To avoid conflict, they often keep their disagreements to themselves.
  • They find it hard to say no and tend to take on too much.
  • They need more time to adjust to sudden changes.
  • They are used to going along with others and sometimes overlook their own needs.

What Work Environment Suits the S Koala Type?

Your behavior style does not decide what job you can do, but it does affect which environment energizes you. S Koala types are usually most comfortable where the pace is steady, team relationships are good, roles are clear, and there is time to adapt to change, such as administration, HR, customer service, education, caregiving and back-office support.

On the other hand, constant change, heated conflict, or being rushed all the time are what most easily wear S Koala types out.

How Do You Communicate with an S Koala Type?

  • Slow your tone down and give them time to think.
  • Avoid rushing them, and do not throw too many changes at them at once.
  • To learn what they really think, ask in private and in a relaxed way.
  • Start by acknowledging the work they quietly put in.

Under pressure: When stressed, S Koala types may withdraw and go quiet, agreeing on the surface while disagreeing inside; if pressure builds up for long, they may also suddenly blow up. This is usually a sign that they need to be asked and heard.

FAQ

Does the S Koala type have no opinions of its own?

No. S Koala types prefer to hear everyone else out first, and often keep their views to themselves to keep the peace. If you ask in a relaxed, unhurried way, they will usually share what they think.

Is a person only one animal?

No. Everyone has all four tendencies, and it is common for two of them to both be strong.

Is the DISC behavior style test accurate?

The DISC behavior style test describes your behavior tendencies based on your own answers. It is useful for getting to know yourself and improving communication, but it is not a measure of ability or a diagnostic tool, and the results are for reference only.
