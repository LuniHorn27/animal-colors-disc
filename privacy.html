這份說明告訴你，使用「測出你的動物本色｜DISC 行為風格測驗」時，我們會收集哪些資料、拿來做什麼、存放在哪裡，以及你可以怎麼查詢或刪除自己的資料。

我們收集哪些資料

  • 作答資料：抽到的題目、每一題的答案和作答時間、作答方式（一題一頁或全部列出）、最後兩頁「選一段最像你的描述」的選擇、「這個結果像你嗎？」的評分，以及根據答案算出的結果。
  • 作答語言：你作答時使用的語言（中文或英文）。
  • 意見回饋（選單裡的「意見回饋」，選填）：你選的「最喜歡的部分」和「測完後的幫助」、1–10 分的推薦意願、你寫的一句話，以及當時使用的語言。不會問你的名字或聯絡方式，也不會和你的作答資料連在一起；只用來改善這個測驗。
  • 基本資料：年齡層、性別、學歷、就業狀態（選「其他」時，是你自己填的說明）、在學／畢業學校、在學／畢業科系為必填。從班級活動連結進入時，不問就業狀態，會自動記為「學生」（見下方班級代碼）。
  • 名字和 Email：名字為必填；Email 為選填。
  • 進一步了解的意願和聯絡方式（選填）：你勾選「想進一步了解」時，我們會記錄這個意願；你也可以留下電話或 LINE ID，讓我們和你聯絡。
  • 班級代碼：從老師提供的活動連結進入時，會自動記錄你參加的是哪一場活動。班級活動連結不問就業狀態，會自動記為「學生」。
  • 登入資料（你選擇登入時）：用 Google 帳號登入時，我們會取得你的 Google 帳號 Email 和名稱；用 Email 登入時，會取得你用來登入的 Email。
  • 防止自動程式的檢查：送出作答或意見回饋前，Cloudflare Turnstile 會檢查瀏覽器資訊和 IP 位址，用來分辨真人和自動程式。我們不會保存這些資訊。
  • 「換個角度看自己」的回覆：你回覆別人的邀請時填的資料，詳見下方〈換個角度看自己（了解他人眼中的你）〉。
  • 存在你裝置上的資料：作答進度、你的結果、深色模式和語言設定、還沒送出的作答，以及「換個角度看自己」的專屬結果連結和你回覆過的結果，會存在你自己的瀏覽器裡，讓你下次打開時可以接著使用。

資料的用途

  • 計算並顯示你的測驗結果。
  • 改善這份測驗：分析題目的品質、測驗的準確度，並建立參考標準。分析時使用不含名字和 Email 的資料。
  • 參加學校或單位舉辦的活動時：你的名字和測驗結果會提供給該學校或單位，用於學生輔導及活動相關用途；帶隊的老師也會看到全班的統計（例如各學歷、就業狀態的人數，以及人數最多的學校和科系），統計中不會列出個別的名字。
  • 你有填 Email 時：未來有其他心理測驗時，寄給你。
  • 你勾選「想進一步了解」並留下電話或 LINE ID 時：由我們和你聯絡，說明職涯方向或測驗結果的進一步解析。電話和 LINE ID 不會提供給學校或單位。
  • 你登入時：保存你的歷次結果。

我們不會出售你的資料，不會拿來做廣告，也不會提供給上面列出的對象以外的人或公司。

Google 帳號資料的使用

用 Google 帳號登入時，我們只會取得你的 Email 和名稱，而且只用來辨識你的帳號、保存你的結果。這些資料不會用於廣告，也不會轉交給其他人。我們使用 Google 帳號資料的方式，遵守 Google API 服務：使用者資料政策，包括其中的「有限使用」規定。

資料存放在哪裡、怎麼保護

  • 作答資料和帳號資料存放在 Supabase 的資料庫，主機位於日本東京；網站由 Cloudflare 提供。
  • 資料在網路上傳送時全程加密（HTTPS）。
  • 名字、Email、電話和 LINE ID 和作答資料分開存放，只有網站管理者能讀取。
  • 資料庫設有權限規則：沒有登入的人讀不到任何作答資料，登入的人只能讀到自己的結果。「換個角度看自己」的邀請人，只能用自己的專屬結果連結（或登入帳號）讀到大家回覆他的結果；拿到邀請連結的人，只看得到邀請人填的暱稱。

換個角度看自己（了解他人眼中的你）

做完測驗後，你可以邀請朋友、家人、老師、同學或同事，從幾組敘述裡選出最像你的敘述，再和你的自評對照。

  • 回覆的人填的資料：和邀請人的關係（朋友、家人、老師、同學、同事，或選「其他」自己填寫，最多 10 個字；英文畫面最多 20 個字）、選填的名字、回覆時使用的語言（中文或英文）、每一題選的「最像」和「最不像」的敘述、每一題的作答時間、根據選擇算出的結果，以及看完結果後選填的「這個測驗像「某某」嗎？」評分。回覆的人不用登入。
  • 用途：產生邀請人的對照結果，以及改善這份測驗的題目（分析時不使用名字）。「這個測驗像「某某」嗎？」的評分只用來改善測驗，不會顯示給邀請人。
  • 邀請人看得到：所有回覆的平均、依關係分組的平均，以及每個人各自選出來的類型（例如「孔雀」）。有留名字的人會顯示名字和關係；沒有留名字的人只顯示「匿名」和選出來的類型，不顯示名字和關係。不過，匿名只代表不顯示名字，不保證完全認不出來：回覆的人很少時（例如某一組只有一個人），平均就等於那個人的回覆；邀請人在新的回覆加入前後各看一次，也能推算出那一筆回覆。
  • 回覆的人看得到：只有自己選出來的結果。回覆的人看不到邀請人自己的測驗結果，也看不到其他人的回覆。
  • 邀請人刪除資料時：邀請人刪除自己的測驗結果時，這份邀請和所有人給他的回覆會一起刪除。
  • 回覆的人要刪除自己的回覆：請寄信到下方的信箱，附上邀請人的暱稱（題目裡稱呼他的名字）、你填的名字（沒填可以省略）、你和邀請人的關係，以及大約的送出日期，我們確認後會刪除那一筆回覆。沒有留名字的回覆，可能無法確認是哪一筆。

保存多久

我們只在達成上面這些用途所需的期間保存資料。你隨時可以要求刪除。

你的權利

依照台灣的《個人資料保護法》，你可以要求查詢、閱覽或取得自己資料的複本，也可以要求補充、更正、停止使用或刪除。

  • 有登入的人：在「我的歷次結果」頁面，每一次結果旁邊都有「刪除」，可以自己刪除任何一次結果。要一次刪除全部資料，或刪除登入帳號本身，請寄信到下方的信箱。
  • 沒有登入的人：請寄信到下方的信箱，附上作答日期，以及作答時填的名字或 Email，方便我們找到你的資料。
  • 回覆過別人邀請的人：請寄信到下方的信箱，附上邀請人的暱稱、你填的名字、你和邀請人的關係，以及大約的送出日期。

聯絡我們

對這份說明有任何問題，或要查詢、刪除資料，請寄信到 a21420000@gmail.com。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This English version is provided for convenience. If it differs from the Chinese version, the Chinese version prevails.

This policy explains what information we collect when you use "What's Your Inner Animal? | DISC Behavioral Style Test," what we use it for, where it is stored, and how you can access or delete your own data.

What we collect

  • Your answers: the questions you were given, your answer and response time for each question, how you answered (one question per page or all questions on one page), your choices on the last two pages ("pick the description that sounds most like you"), your rating of "Does this analysis sound like you?", and the result calculated from your answers.
  • Language: the language you used to take the test (Chinese or English).
  • Feedback ("Feedback" in the menu, optional): the parts you liked best, how the test helped you, your 1–10 likelihood to recommend, anything you write in your own words, and the language you used. We do not ask for your name or contact details, and this is not linked to your answers. It is used only to improve this test.
  • Basic information: age group, gender, education, employment status (if you choose "Other," the description you type yourself), school and major (current or graduated) are required.
  • Name and email: your name is required; email is optional.
  • Interest in learning more, and contact details (optional): if you check the box to learn more, we record that you are interested. You can also leave a phone number or LINE ID so we can contact you.
  • Class code: if you open the test from an activity link provided by a teacher, we automatically record which activity you joined.
  • Sign-in information (if you choose to sign in): when you sign in with Google, we receive the email address and name of your Google account. When you sign in with email, we receive the email address you use to sign in.
  • Bot check: before your answers or feedback are sent, Cloudflare Turnstile checks browser information and your IP address to tell people apart from automated programs. We do not keep this information.
  • Data stored on your device: your progress, your result, your dark mode and language settings, and any answers not yet sent are stored in your own browser so you can continue next time.

How we use your data

  • To calculate and show your test result.
  • To improve this test: we analyze the quality of the questions and the accuracy of the test, and build reference standards. These analyses use data without names or email addresses.
  • When you take part in an activity run by a school or organization: your name and test result will be provided to that school or organization for student guidance and activity-related purposes. The teacher leading the activity can also see statistics for the whole class (for example, how many people have each education level or employment status, and the most common schools and majors); the statistics do not list individual names.
  • If you enter your email: to send you other psychological assessments in the future.
  • If you check the box to learn more and leave a phone number or LINE ID: to contact you about career directions or a more detailed explanation of your result. Your phone number and LINE ID will not be given to schools or organizations.
  • If you sign in: to save your past results.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or give it to any person or company other than those listed above.

Use of Google account data

When you sign in with Google, we only receive your email address and name, and we use them only to identify your account and save your results. This data is not used for advertising and is not passed on to anyone else. Our use of Google account data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where your data is stored and how it is protected

  • Your answers and account data are stored in a Supabase database hosted in Tokyo, Japan. The website is served by Cloudflare.
  • All data is encrypted while it is sent over the internet (HTTPS).
  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, and LINE IDs are stored separately from your answers, and only the site administrator can read them.
  • The database has access rules: people who are not signed in cannot read any data, and signed-in users can only read their own results. For "See Yourself from a Different Angle," the person who sent the invitation can read the replies to it only through their own private results link (or by signing in), and anyone who opens the invitation link can only see the nickname the inviter entered.

See Yourself from a Different Angle (see how others see you)

After the test, you can invite friends, family, teachers, classmates, or coworkers to pick the statements that are most like you and least like you from a few sets, and then compare their answers with how you described yourself.

  • What the people who reply enter: their relationship to the inviter (friend, family, teacher, classmate, coworker, or "Other," which they fill in themselves, up to 20 characters), an optional name, the language they used to reply (Chinese or English), the "most like" and "least like" statement they pick for each question, the time they spend on each question, the result calculated from their choices, and an optional rating of "Does this sound like [name]?" after they see the result. They do not need to sign in.
  • What we use it for: to produce the inviter's comparison result, and to improve the questions of this test (names are not used in this analysis). The "Does this sound like [name]?" rating is used only to improve the test and is not shown to the inviter.
  • What the inviter can see: the average of all replies, the average for each relationship group, and the type each person picked (for example "Peacock"). People who left a name are shown with their name and relationship. People who did not leave a name are shown only as "Anonymous" with the type they picked, without a name or relationship. However, anonymous only means the name is not shown, and it does not guarantee that no one can tell who replied: when very few people reply (for example, only one person in a group), the average is that person's reply, and an inviter who looks before and after a new reply arrives can work out that reply.
  • What the people who reply can see: only their own result. They cannot see the inviter's own test result or anyone else's reply.
  • When the inviter deletes their data: when an inviter deletes their test result, the invitation and all replies to it are deleted too.
  • If you replied and want your reply deleted: please email the address below with the inviter's nickname (the name used in the questions), the name you entered (leave it out if you did not enter one), your relationship to the inviter, and the approximate date you sent it. After we confirm it, we will delete that reply. We may not be able to find a reply that has no name.

How long we keep your data

We keep data only for as long as needed for the purposes above. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your rights

Under Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act, you can ask to look up, view, or get a copy of your data, and you can ask us to add to, correct, stop using, or delete it.

  • If you have signed in: on the "My past results" page, there is a "Delete" button next to each result, so you can delete any result yourself. To delete all your data at once, or the account itself, please email the address below.
  • If you have not signed in: please email the address below with the date you took the test and the name or email you entered, so we can find your data.
  • If you replied to someone else's invitation: please email the address below with the inviter's nickname, the name you entered, your relationship to the inviter, and the approximate date you sent it.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy, or want to access or delete your data, please email a21420000@gmail.com.
